# Current manuscript and release boundary

Scientific version: **31 October 2026**. Release preparation recorded **1 November 2026**.

## Title and contribution

**CalligraGuard: Source-Dependency-Balanced Adaptation for Glyph Inspection**

The method uses trusted native source dependencies to prevent repeated descendants of a few altered records from dominating acquisition adaptation and causing false alarms on unaffected outputs. It combines a compact shared acquisition model, source-group weighting, robust fitting, and representative same-panel session calibration. Group weighting, Huber estimation, and median aggregation are conventional ingredients. The contribution is the source-dependency construction, the explicit collateral-alarm endpoint, its conditional influence analysis, and controlled matched evidence.

The distinct-glyph diagnostic is post-hoc. The qualified fault population is reused development evidence. Glyphs, recipes, origins, and edit magnitudes are clustered, not independent repair events. The pool-median comparator also produces zero collateral flags and has one additional altered-target detection. No unique superiority over that comparator is claimed.

Other-panel calibration gives 108/800 source-balanced normal-session flags. Same-panel qualification is a required information condition, not a universal false-alarm guarantee. Historical checks give a visible component-repair tie and a correction below raster tolerance; the Liberation anchor revision remains outside isolated-outline evaluation. These cases do not establish natural-event superiority or designer-time benefits.
