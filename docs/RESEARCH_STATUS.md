## Decision

The configured computational work is complete. Preserve the completed v3 comparison and use ordinary alignment, sparse fitting and direct source/dependency comparison as strong controls. ## Information and independent evidence

Source-available QA receives both revisions. Raster-only explanation does not. All compared methods within a track must receive equal acquisition/source information; never aggregate the tracks into one undifferentiated leaderboard.

Related masters, repeated sizes, sequential fixes and mirrored repositories are not independent repair events. Historical commits provide provenance, not independent expert judgments. Controlled translations are intentional by construction, not a corpus of naturally adjudicated legitimate revisions.

## Not completed and not claimed

- No final novelty/superiority claim supported by the exploratory branches.
